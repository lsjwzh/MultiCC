# MultiCC Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

**Effective date / 生效日期: September 2, 2026 / 2026 年 9 月 2 日**

MultiCC is a client for a MultiCC server that you or your organization operate. The MultiCC app developer does not collect, sell, use for advertising, or use for tracking any personal data through the iOS app.

MultiCC 是连接到由您或您的组织自行运行的 MultiCC 服务器的客户端。MultiCC 应用开发者不会通过 iOS 应用收集或出售任何个人数据，也不会将个人数据用于广告或追踪。

## Information processed by the app / 应用处理的信息

The app may process the following information only to provide features you request:

- The address and access token of your MultiCC server, plus app preferences such as language and text size. These are stored locally on your device.
- Messages, terminal content, voice input, and files or photos you explicitly choose. These are sent only to the MultiCC server you configure so that it can perform the requested operation.
- Local-network discovery information used to find a nearby MultiCC server.
- Notification state used to show local notifications on your device.

应用仅会为提供您主动使用的功能而处理以下信息：

- 您的 MultiCC 服务器地址和访问令牌，以及语言、文字大小等应用偏好；这些内容保存在您的设备本地。
- 您主动提交的消息、终端内容、语音输入，以及选择的文件或照片；这些内容仅发送到您配置的 MultiCC 服务器，以执行您请求的操作。
- 用于发现附近 MultiCC 服务器的本地网络信息。
- 用于在设备上显示本地通知的通知状态。

## Permissions / 权限

MultiCC may request the following permissions when needed:

- **Local Network:** to discover and connect to a MultiCC server on your network.
- **Microphone:** for voice input and real-time voice features.
- **Photos:** to attach a photo that you explicitly select.
- **Notifications:** to notify you about session events on your device.

MultiCC 可能会在需要时请求以下权限：

- **本地网络：**发现并连接到您网络中的 MultiCC 服务器。
- **麦克风：**提供语音输入和实时语音功能。
- **照片：**附加您主动选择的照片。
- **通知：**在您的设备上通知会话事件。

You can deny or revoke these permissions in iOS Settings. MultiCC does not access your location, contacts, advertising identifier, or photo library unless you initiate the relevant feature.

您可以在 iOS“设置”中拒绝或撤销这些权限。MultiCC 不会访问您的位置、通讯录或广告标识符；只有在您主动使用相关功能时才会访问您选择的照片。

## Self-hosted servers and AI providers / 自托管服务器与 AI 服务商

Your MultiCC server is controlled by you or your organization, not by the app developer. Data sent to that server is governed by the server operator's policies and configuration. If the server connects to third-party AI or developer-tool providers, those providers may process content under their own terms and privacy policies. Review the policies of your server operator and configured providers before sending sensitive information.

您的 MultiCC 服务器由您或您的组织控制，而非由应用开发者控制。发送到该服务器的数据受服务器运营方的政策和配置约束。如果服务器连接第三方 AI 或开发工具服务商，这些服务商可能依据各自的条款与隐私政策处理内容。发送敏感信息前，请查阅服务器运营方及所配置服务商的相关政策。

## Retention and deletion / 保留与删除

The developer does not receive or retain app data. You can remove locally stored server details and preferences by using the app's controls or deleting the app. For content retained by your configured MultiCC server or a third-party provider, contact that server operator or provider.

开发者不会接收或保留应用数据。您可以通过应用内功能或删除应用来移除设备本地保存的服务器信息和偏好。对于由您配置的 MultiCC 服务器或第三方服务商保留的内容，请联系相应的服务器运营方或服务商。

## Children / 儿童

MultiCC is a developer tool and is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

MultiCC 是开发者工具，并非面向儿童。开发者不会有意通过应用收集儿童的个人信息。
